# Contributing to AssilOS

Thanks for checking out AssilOS! Here's how you can help.

---

## 🐛 Found a Bug?

1. **Check** if it's already reported in [Issues](https://github.com/assilassilkerr-boop/AssilOS/issues)
2. If not, **open a new issue** and include:
   - What happened
   - What you expected to happen
   - Your setup (QEMU, real hardware, etc.)

---

## 💡 Have an Idea?

Open an issue and tell me about it! I'm always open to suggestions.

---

## 🔧 Want to Contribute Code?

1. **Fork** the repository
2. **Make your changes**
3. **Test** with QEMU: `qemu-system-x86_64 -fda os.img`
4. **Submit a Pull Request**

---

## 📋 Guidelines

- Keep it simple (remember, this is a 512-byte boot sector!)
- Test your changes before submitting
- Credit original authors where due
- Be respectful and constructive

---

## ❓ Questions?

Just open an issue and ask!
